Practical Guidance

Doorstep or workshop?

Doorstep may be suitable when

  • Issue is minor or diagnostic
  • Vehicle is parked safely
  • Battery or small checks are needed
  • You need guidance before moving the car

Workshop may be better when

  • Major repair or lift work is needed
  • Parts replacement requires equipment
  • Safety systems need detailed inspection
  • The issue repeats after minor support

Can you inspect before towing?
