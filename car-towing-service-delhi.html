When To Call

Towing when driving further is not worth the risk.

The first decision is whether the car can move safely. If not, towing is the responsible next step.

01

04

Tyre, rim or wheel damage makes rolling unsafe.

Practical Guidance

Repair roadside or tow?

Roadside support may help when

  • Issue is minor and safe to inspect
  • Battery or puncture problem is manageable
  • Vehicle is parked safely
  • No major warning signs are present

Towing may be safer when

  • Brakes, steering or suspension are affected
  • Engine overheating or severe leakage appears
  • Vehicle cannot roll or move safely
  • Accident damage is visible
