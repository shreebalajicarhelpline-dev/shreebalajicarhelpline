When To Call

Tyre help when driving further may not be safe.

The goal is to avoid tyre damage, rim damage and unsafe driving after air loss.

01

Sudden air loss

The tyre loses air quickly while driving or after parking.

02

Visible puncture

Nail, screw or sharp object visible in the tyre tread.

03

Spare wheel support

Help needed with spare wheel change or checking whether it is safe.

04

Slow leak

Tyre pressure drops repeatedly after filling air.

05

Roadside tyre trouble

Vehicle is stopped on the road due to tyre pressure or puncture.

06

Unsure if safe

Call before driving further if the tyre looks low or damaged.

Practical Guidance

Repair, spare wheel or towing?

Puncture support may help when

  • Tubeless tyre has a nail or screw
  • Tyre pressure is low but sidewall looks safe
  • Spare wheel is available
  • Vehicle is parked safely

Towing or replacement may be safer when

  • Sidewall is cut or tyre is badly damaged
  • Rim damage is visible
  • No safe place to work
  • Vehicle cannot move without risk

Puncture Repair FAQs

Can I drive with a puncture?

Avoid driving if the tyre is visibly low or flat. It may damage the tyre and rim.

What should I share on WhatsApp?

Share location, tyre photo if possible, and whether a spare wheel is available.

Do you help with spare wheel change?

Yes, guidance or support can be provided depending on the situation.

What if the tyre sidewall is damaged?

Sidewall damage is usually unsafe for repair and may need replacement or towing.

Can you help in parking areas?

Yes, support can be arranged for home, office and parking locations.
