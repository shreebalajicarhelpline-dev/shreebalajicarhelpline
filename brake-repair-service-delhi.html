When To Stop And Check

Brake issues should never be ignored.

Brakes are safety-critical. If braking feels different, call before driving further.

01

Grinding noise

Metallic sound while braking can indicate worn components.

02

Squealing sound

High-pitched noise may need pad or surface inspection.

03

Weak braking

Pedal feels soft, car takes longer to stop or braking feels unsafe.

04

Vibration

Steering or pedal vibration while braking.

05

Brake warning light

Dashboard warning or low fluid concern.

06

Burning smell

Heat or smell after braking needs attention.

Practical Guidance

Drive, inspect or tow?

Inspection may be possible when

  • Noise is mild and vehicle is parked
  • Brake pedal feels normal
  • No fluid leak is visible
  • Warning lights are not severe

Do not drive when

  • Pedal sinks or feels unsafe
  • Fluid leakage is visible
  • Braking distance is reduced
  • Grinding or severe vibration appears

Helpful Answers

Brake Repair FAQs

Can I drive with brake noise?

Avoid driving if the noise is grinding, braking feels weak or vibration is severe.

What should I share when calling?

Share the sound, pedal feel, warning lights and whether there is any fluid leak.

Do brake pads always need replacement?

Not always. Inspection is needed to understand pads, discs, fluid and related parts.

What if the brake pedal feels soft?

Treat it as safety-critical and call before driving further.

Can towing be needed for brake problems?

Yes, if braking is unsafe, towing may be the safer option.
