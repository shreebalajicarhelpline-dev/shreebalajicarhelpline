When To Call

AC issues need diagnosis, not guesswork.

The first principle is simple: weak cooling does not always mean “fill gas.” The real issue may be gas loss, leakage, filter blockage, compressor behaviour, blower weakness or electrical control.

01

Weak cooling

AC is running but the cabin does not become cool enough.

02

Cooling drops again

Gas was filled earlier but cooling reduced again, indicating possible leakage or deeper issue.

03

Low airflow

Blower runs weakly or air from vents is not strong enough.

04

Bad smell

Unpleasant smell when AC starts or while the cabin is cooling.

05

Compressor concern

Noise, frequent cut-off, AC not engaging or unusual load while AC is on.

06

Cabin fogging

Fogging, inconsistent temperature or poor defogging during driving.

Practical Guidance

Gas refill or AC repair?

Gas refill may help when

  • Cooling reduced gradually
  • No obvious leak symptoms appear
  • System otherwise behaves normally
  • Inspection suggests low refrigerant only

Repair may be needed when

  • Cooling fails again quickly after refill
  • Compressor noise or frequent cut-off appears
  • Leak signs are visible
  • Blower, condenser or electrical controls misbehave

Car AC Repair FAQs

Why is my car AC not cooling?

It may be low gas, leakage, clogged filter, compressor issue, blower problem, condenser concern or electrical control issue.

Is gas filling always enough?

No. If cooling drops again, leakage or system repair may be needed. That is why check-up comes before paid work.

Is car AC check-up free?

Yes. Basic AC check-up is free. Gas, parts, leak repair or detailed service are paid only after confirmation.

What should I share when calling?

Share car model, cooling behaviour, airflow, smell, AC noise and whether gas was filled recently.

Can AC issues be inspected at doorstep?

Some checks may be guided or inspected on-location, while deeper AC repairs may need workshop support.
