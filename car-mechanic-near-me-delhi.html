Practical Guidance

On-location support or workshop?

On-location help may be suitable when

  • The issue is minor or diagnostic
  • Vehicle is parked safely
  • Battery, tyre or basic checks are needed
  • You need guidance before driving

Workshop may be needed when

  • Major mechanical repair is required
  • Special equipment is needed
  • Repeated fault needs deeper diagnosis
  • Safety-critical parts are affected
