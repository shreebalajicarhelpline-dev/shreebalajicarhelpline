Practical Guidance

Touch-up or repaint?

Minor work may help when

  • Scratch is superficial
  • Dent is small and accessible
  • Paint layer is mostly intact
  • Bumper scuff is light

Larger repair may be needed when

  • Panel is deeply dented
  • Paint has cracked or peeled
  • Bumper is misaligned or cracked
  • Colour blending is required

Helpful Answers

Denting & Painting FAQs

Should I send photos first?

Yes. Photos help understand the dent, scratch and repair scope.

Can small scratches be touched up?

Some light scratches may be handled with minor work, while deeper paint damage may need repainting.

Will colour match be discussed?

Yes, finish expectations and scope should be clear before work begins.

Can bumper damage be repaired?

It depends on crack, alignment and paint condition.

Is denting and painting emergency work?

Usually it is planned work unless damage affects safe driving.
